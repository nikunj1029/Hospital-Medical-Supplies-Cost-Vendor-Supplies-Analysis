# Hospital-Medical-Supplies-Cost-Vendor-Supplies-Analysis

Project Overview

This project is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze hospital medical supply purchases, vendor performance, pricing trends, and cost-saving opportunities. The dashboard enables hospital management to monitor purchasing activities, compare contract prices with market prices, and identify high-cost products through dynamic visualizations and filters.

⸻

 Business Problem

Hospitals purchase medicines and medical supplies from multiple vendors across different departments. Without centralized reporting, it is difficult to:

* Monitor purchase costs.
* Compare vendor pricing.
* Identify expensive products.
* Track departmental spending.
* Discover potential cost-saving opportunities.

⸻

 Solution

Developed a 2-page interactive Excel dashboard that transforms raw procurement data into actionable insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Excel formulas.

The dashboard helps decision-makers:

* Monitor overall purchasing costs.
* Evaluate vendor performance.
* Compare contract prices with market prices.
* Analyze category-wise spending and savings.
* Identify high-cost products and hospitals.

⸻

 Dashboard Features

Page 1 – Executive Overview

* Total Purchase Cost
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Purchased
* Average Product Price
* Total Vendors
* Spend by Product Category
* Top 10 Expensive Products
* Department-wise Spending
* Purchase Cost by Hospital
* Monthly Purchase Trend

Page 2 – Vendor & Price Analysis

* Total Savings
* Average Contract Price
* Average Market Price
* Vendor-wise Purchase Cost
* Contract Price vs Market Price
* Savings by Product Category

 Key Insights

* Analyzed 942 purchase transactions across 8 hospitals, 8 vendors, and 4 product categories.
* Evaluated ₹2.46 Cr+ in medical supply purchases.
* Identified ₹45.1 L+ in potential cost savings through contract price analysis.
* Highlighted high-spending hospitals, expensive products, and top-performing vendors to support cost optimization.

⸻

 Business Impact

The dashboard enables procurement and hospital management teams to make data-driven purchasing decisions, improve vendor negotiations, reduce unnecessary expenses, and enhance operational transparency through interactive reporting.
